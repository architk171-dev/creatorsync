Part 1 — Product Requirements

1. Vision & Problem Statement

Problem: Brands spend $21B+ annually on influencer marketing, yet 25–40% of influencer audiences are estimated to be fake or inactive. Brand managers lack efficient tools to verify creator authenticity, estimate fair pricing, predict campaign ROI, or compare creators across platforms before committing budget.

Solution: NoCap Verify is an AI-powered due diligence platform that generates verification reports, trust scores, pricing estimates, and campaign-ready briefs — giving brands and agencies the data they need to make confident influencer investment decisions.

Core Value Proposition: Enter a creator handle → get a comprehensive verification report with trust scoring, fake follower detection, engagement auditing, pricing benchmarks, and ROI projections — in under 60 seconds.

2. Target Personas

Brand Manager

Marketing lead at a D2C or enterprise brand running influencer campaigns.

  • Needs to verify creators before signing contracts
  • Must justify influencer spend to leadership
  • Wants ROI projections and pricing benchmarks
  • Manages 5–50 creator partnerships per quarter

PR / Talent Agency

Agency managing multiple brand accounts and creator rosters.

  • Runs multi-brand campaigns simultaneously
  • Needs to match creators to brand briefs at scale
  • Requires competitive intelligence on rival campaigns
  • Values speed and bulk verification

Creator / Manager

Content creator or their talent manager seeking brand deals.

  • Wants to know their own trust score and market rate
  • Needs a shareable verification report as proof of legitimacy
  • Interested in competitive positioning vs. peers

3. Feature Inventory

3.1 Creator Verification Engine

Instagram + YouTube Report Generation Live

What: User enters a YouTube handle/URL and/or Instagram handle(s). The system fetches real data from both platforms, runs a proprietary audit algorithm, and generates a comprehensive report.

Who it's for: Brand managers verifying a creator before signing. Agencies vetting talent roster. Creators checking their own standing.

Report includes:

  • Trust Score (0–100) — composite score based on engagement-to-follower ratio, growth patterns, and content consistency
  • Verdict — Trusted / Caution / Suspicious with specific flags (e.g., "engagement rate below category benchmark," "suspicious follower spike")
  • Engagement audit — likes, comments, views analyzed against category benchmarks
  • Pricing estimate — market rate by platform and content type (post, story, reel, video) based on follower tier
  • ROI projections — estimated impressions, CPM, cost-per-engagement
  • Recent content analysis — last 10–20 posts/videos with individual performance metrics
  • Multi-handle support — enter multiple Instagram handles in one request

Twitter / X Verification Live

What: Enter a Twitter handle to get follower audit, engagement analysis, and verification status check.

Who it's for: Brands evaluating Twitter-native creators or verifying cross-platform presence.

LinkedIn Verification Live

What: Enter a LinkedIn profile to audit professional influence — connections, engagement, professional credibility.

Who it's for: B2B brands and agencies targeting professional/thought-leader creators.

3.2 AI Campaign Planner

Campaign Brief Analysis & Creator Matching Live

What: User enters a structured campaign brief (brand name, category, budget, goals, target audience, platforms). Gemini AI analyzes the brief, then the system searches for matching creators across YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn. Each creator is scored for brand fit.

Who it's for: Brand managers planning a new campaign. Agencies pitching creator lists to clients.

Brief includes:

  • Multi-goal support (awareness, conversions, content, community)
  • Platform selection (YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)
  • Budget range and timeline
  • Target audience demographics
  • Brand guidelines and anti-AI detection prompts

Output: Ranked list of creator recommendations with fit scores, estimated costs, audience overlap analysis, and suggested content formats.

Deliverable Brief Generation Live

What: For any matched creator, generate a detailed deliverable brief with scripts, content directions, posting schedules, and budget allocation.

Who it's for: Brand managers who need ready-to-send briefs for selected creators.

3.3 Competitor Intelligence

Competitor Campaign Tracker Live

What: Enter 1–3 competitor brand names and a category. Gemini AI identifies which creators those brands have worked with, their content strategies, and estimated spend.

Who it's for: Brand strategists benchmarking against competitors. Agencies building competitive pitch decks.

3.4 Martech Features

Creative Performance Benchmarks Live

Industry benchmarks by category and platform — engagement rates, view rates, growth rates — used as baselines in verification reports and campaign scoring.

Campaign Performance Predictor Live

ROI projections (impressions, CPM, cost-per-engagement) calculated per creator based on their actual metrics and the campaign's budget.

Content Format Scoring Live

Analysis of which content formats (reels, stories, long-form video, static posts) perform best for each creator based on historical data.

Creator Pricing Engine Live

Market-rate pricing estimates by platform, follower tier (Nano through Mega), and content type. Integrated into verification reports.

3.5 Platform & Infrastructure

Authentication Live

NextAuth with Google OAuth and email/password. JWT sessions. User records stored in Supabase. Sign-in button currently hidden (awaiting Google OAuth credentials on production).

Event Tracking / Analytics Live

Server-side event logging to Supabase events table. Tracks: report generation, campaign analysis, deep report requests, sign-ups, errors with stack traces. Client-side fire-and-forget tracking via /api/events.

Rate Limiting & Caching Live

In-memory per-IP rate limiting (10 req/min per endpoint). In-memory response cache with TTL expiration. Resets on deploy.

Payments (Razorpay) Placeholder

API routes stubbed for Razorpay order creation and webhook handling. Not yet wired.

4. User Flows

Flow 1: Creator Verification

01User lands on homepage → scrolls to "Verify" section or clicks "Verify Creator" in navbar
02Enters YouTube channel URL/handle + Instagram handle(s) + email in GenerateForm
03System resolves YouTube channel via Data API → fetches channel stats + recent 20 videos
04System fetches Instagram profile via Apify scraper → follower count, engagement, recent posts
05Audit engine calculates trust score (engagement ratio, growth patterns, category benchmarks)
06Pricing engine estimates market rates by tier. ROI engine projects campaign performance
07ReportPreview renders full report inline: trust score, verdict, flags, pricing, videos, engagement charts
08Event tracked to Supabase: report_generated with creator handle + platform

Flow 2: Campaign Planning

01User navigates to /campaign → fills campaign brief form (brand, category, budget, goals, platforms, audience)
02Form state auto-saved to sessionStorage (survives browser back/forward)
03POST to /api/campaign → Gemini analyzes brief → extracts search queries and matching criteria
04Discover engine searches creators across selected platforms (YouTube API search, Apify for IG/Twitter/LinkedIn)
05Gemini scores each creator for brand fit (audience alignment, content style, engagement quality)
06Results displayed as ranked creator cards with fit scores, estimated costs, suggested formats
07User clicks "Generate Brief" on any creator → POST to /api/campaign/brief → Gemini produces deliverable brief with scripts, schedules, budgets
08Events tracked: campaign_analyzed, deep_report_generated

Flow 3: Competitor Intelligence

01User navigates to /competitor → enters 1–3 competitor brand names + industry category
02POST to /api/competitor → Gemini generates analysis of each brand's influencer strategy
03Returns: identified creators per brand, estimated spend, content strategy breakdown, collaboration types
04Results displayed in structured comparison view

Flow 4: Twitter / LinkedIn Verification

01User navigates to /twitter or /linkedin
02Enters handle → POST to /api/twitter or /api/linkedin
03Apify fetches profile data → audit engine scores → report rendered

6.2 Request Flow Architecture

API Pattern: Every API route follows the same structure: rate limit check → cache check → fetch external data → process/score → track event → return JSON response. Errors are caught, logged to Supabase via logError(), and return structured error responses.

6.3 AI Pipeline (Gemini)

The Gemini integration uses a 3-model fallback chain for resilience:

  1. gemini-3.5-flash (primary — fast, capable)
  2. gemini-3.5-flash-lite (fallback — lighter)
  3. gemini-flash-latest (last resort)

All Gemini responses are parsed with safeParseJSON() which handles common LLM output issues: trailing commas, control characters, unescaped newlines inside strings.

events id, event_name, user_id, email, properties (jsonb), error_message, endpoint, request_body, stack_trace, created_at Analytics events and error logs

All tables have Row-Level Security (RLS) enabled with appropriate policies. Indexes on events.event_name, events.created_at, users.email.
